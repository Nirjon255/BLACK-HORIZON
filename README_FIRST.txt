BLACK HORIZON — GODOT 4 CAMPAIGN PROTOTYPE

IMPORT
1. Extract this ZIP to a folder.
2. Open Godot 4.x and choose Import.
3. Select project.godot inside this folder.
4. Choose Import & Edit, then press Play.

CONTROLS
WASD move | Mouse look | Left click fire | R reload
E interact | Shift sprint | Ctrl crouch | Space jump
V toggle first-/third-person camera | Esc release mouse
Click the game window to capture the mouse again.

MISSION LOOP
Choose an unlocked mission. Find the glowing intel object and press E.
Then reach the red extraction marker and press E. Completing a mission
unlocks the next campaign entry for the current play session.

IMPORTANT SCOPE NOTE
This is a playable campaign foundation/prototype, not a finished AAA
commercial game. All eight campaign entries have briefings and unlock
progression, but currently share one prototype tactical environment and
core objective loop. Environment and soldiers use procedural placeholder
meshes. Detailed models, animation, bespoke mission maps, voice acting,
soundtrack, cinematic story sequences, advanced squad AI, save/load,
controller/mobile support, and final QA remain future development.
The graphics direction is cinematic and atmospheric, but asset fidelity
is intentionally modest for compatibility with lower-end laptops.

Recommended: Godot 4.x Compatibility renderer, 1280x720 or lower.
